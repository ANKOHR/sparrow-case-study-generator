# Lessons for KOML

The domain needs evidence quality and permission gates before prose. Completion
alone does not make work publishable. A useful case study can truthfully have no
measured savings, and a paired measurement can show a regression.

Validated by synthetic planted ground truth: deterministic rules find the intended
eligible cases and reconcile signed decimal totals exactly. A changed source
measurement changes the report. This validates computation, not real source truth
or customer willingness to pay. The assumption that customers will use this remains
unmeasured until the two-week trial.

Operating rule: pair every savings claim with a baseline, outcome, unit, source,
and provenance label. Keep hypothetical values outside measured totals, and record
exclusions so missing evidence never becomes an invented success.
